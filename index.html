Why I teach

言葉が分かると、知らなかった世界が開かれる。

Language opened my world

Learning English took me to university abroad, to friends from many cultures, and to chances I never imagined. I want everyone to feel that, through Japanese.

Everyone blooms differently

No two learners grow the same way. We learn at your pace, with lots of speaking practice and gentle corrections, so you can enjoy talking to people in Japanese.

Before you send
  • When you send a picture to Daisy, it may be shared in the Art gallery on this website, together with your sentence and nickname.
  • Daisy checks every picture first. Not every picture will be shared.
  • Please send only your own work. Use a nickname, and don't include your full name, address, school name or a photo of your face.
  • Under 18? Please ask a parent or guardian for permission before you send.
  • Changed your mind? Send Daisy a message any time, and your picture will be taken down.
