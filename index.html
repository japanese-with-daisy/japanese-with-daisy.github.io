I hold Bachelor's degrees in Education and Psychology, and a Master's in Comparative Education from UCL. I have devoted my life to seeking better education.

Why I teach

言葉が分かると、知らなかった世界が開かれる。

Language opened my world

Learning English took me to university abroad, to friends from many cultures, and to chances I never imagined. I want everyone to feel that, through Japanese.

Everyone blooms differently

No two learners grow the same way. We learn at your pace, with lots of speaking practice and gentle corrections, so you can enjoy talking to people in Japanese.

JLPT N5 · N4

Send your drawing

  1. Draw something you love about Japan — on paper, or on a phone or computer.
  2. Save it as a JPEG or PNG. A clear photo of a paper drawing is fine too.
  3. Send it to Daisy with the button below, together with your username and a sentence about your drawing.

Send your drawing to Daisy

The form opens in a new tab. You'll need to be signed in to a Google account to upload your drawing. Drawings only, please — not photographs.

Before you send
  • Please send a drawing, not a photograph.
  • When you send a drawing to Daisy, it may be shared in the Art gallery on this website, together with your username and message.
  • Daisy checks every drawing first. Not every drawing will be shared.
  • Please send only your own work. Use a username, and don't include your address, school name or your face.
  • Under 18? Please ask a parent or guardian for permission before you send.
  • Changed your mind? Send Daisy a message any time, and your drawing will be taken down.
